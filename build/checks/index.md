## The 12 checks, one step each

 Tick a box when the check passes. Your ticks stay on this device.

 
 
 1
 
 
### Google your business name plus &ldquo;reviews&rdquo;

 Search your business name with the word reviews after it. Look at the very first result.

 **A fail looks like:** the first thing is not yours: a directory, an old listing, or someone else. **First fix:** claim and fill out your Google Business Profile, then ask your next few happy customers to review you there.

 Passed
 
 
 
 2
 
 
### Ask ChatGPT for the best in your city, three ways

 Ask: &ldquo;Who is the best [your trade] in [your city]?&rdquo; Then &ldquo;Who should I hire for [the job] near [your city]?&rdquo; Then &ldquo;Recommend a [your trade] in [your city] with good reviews.&rdquo;

 **A fail looks like:** your name is in none of the three. **First fix:** the three website checks on [the Get Found page](/found/).

 Passed
 
 
 
 3
 
 
### Open your website on your phone with the wifi off

 Turn wifi off so you are on cell data, open your site, and count the seconds until you can actually read it.

 **A fail looks like:** more than about three seconds, or the page jumps around while it loads. A lot of people leave before then. **First fix:** big photos are the most common cause. Shrink those first.

 Passed
 
 
 
 4
 
 
### Search Google Maps from one town over

 Open Google Maps, move the map to the next town you serve, and search for what you do.

 **A fail looks like:** you disappear. **First fix:** add that town to the service area on your Google profile, and give it its own page on your site ([the town page template](/towns/)).

 Passed
 
 
 
 5
 
 
### Look up your biggest competitor in the Meta Ad Library

 Go to facebook.com/ads/library, set your country, and search their name. It is free and public.

 **What you get:** every ad they are running and the date each one started. **First move:** the ones that have run the longest are the ones they keep paying for. [The Ad Library Teardown](/playbook/ad-library-teardown/) walks through it.

 Passed
 
 
 
 6
 
 
### Call your own business number after hours

 After you close, call your business from a phone that is not yours. Listen to what a customer hears.

 **A fail looks like:** it rings out, the voicemail box is full, or the greeting does not say your name or your hours. **First fix:** record a greeting with your business name, your hours and the fastest way to reach you.

 Passed
 
 
 
 7
 
 
### Fill out your own contact form

 Use a different email, fill out the form like a customer would, and time how long until a person answers.

 **A fail looks like:** nobody answers the same day, or the message never arrives at all. **First fix:** send form messages to a phone someone actually checks, and turn on an instant &ldquo;got it, here is what happens next&rdquo; reply.

 Passed
 
 
 
 8
 
 
### Ask ChatGPT what your business does

 Ask: &ldquo;What does [your business name] in [your city] do?&rdquo;

 **A fail looks like:** wrong, out of date (an old address, a service you dropped), or made up. **First fix:** put the facts on your website in plain words and as data, check 2 on [the Get Found page](/found/).

 Passed
 
 
 
 9
 
 
### Check your Google listing address matches your website

 Open your Google listing and your website side by side. Compare the address character by character, then the phone and the hours.

 **A fail looks like:** any difference at all: a missing suite number, an old address, different hours. **First fix:** [the listing match checklist](/match/), address line first.

 Passed
 
 
 
 10
 
 
### Search &ldquo;[your trade] near me&rdquo; in a private window

 Open a private or incognito tab so Google does not use your history. Search, then count how many businesses sit above you.

 **A fail looks like:** you are not in the map results or not on the first screen. **First fix:** Google says it ranks local results by relevance, distance and prominence. You control relevance (every category and service filled in on your profile) and a lot of prominence (reviews and photos).

 Passed
 
 
 
 11
 
 
### Look at your last ten Google reviews

 Count how many of your last ten reviews have a reply from you.

 **A fail looks like:** most of them have no reply. **First fix:** reply to every one, good and bad, short and specific. Then run them through [the review prompt](/reviews/) to see what keeps coming up.

 Passed
 
 
 
 12
 
 
### Ask ChatGPT for your phone number

 Ask: &ldquo;What is the phone number for [your business name] in [your city]?&rdquo;

 **A fail looks like:** a wrong number, an old one, or one it made up. **First fix:** make the number the same everywhere it shows up online: your website, Google, Facebook, Yelp.

 Passed
