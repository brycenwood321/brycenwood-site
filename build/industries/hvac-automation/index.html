The HVAC Problem

You got into HVAC because you are good at it. Not because you wanted to manage CRMs, chase leads, and remember which customer needs a follow-up call.

Every HVAC company hits the same wall. The phone rings during peak season and nobody can answer fast enough. Leads come in from Google, your website, and referrals -- but half of them never get a callback because your office manager is already buried. The customer who called about an AC replacement on Tuesday does not hear back until Friday, and by then they have already called your competitor.

In the off-season, the opposite problem hits. The phone stops ringing. Revenue drops. You scramble to fill the schedule. Your techs are sitting around. And the customers you serviced six months ago -- the ones who would happily book a maintenance visit if someone reminded them -- never hear from you again.

Then there is the marketing problem. You know you should be posting on social media. You know your Google reviews matter. You know your website is outdated and your competitors are showing up above you in search results. But when are you supposed to do that? Between the 7 AM emergency furnace call and the 6 PM duct install?

These are not HVAC problems. They are systems problems. And systems problems have systems solutions.

Built for How HVAC Actually Works

This is not a generic business tool. Every system is designed around the specific realities of running an HVAC company.

Seasonal Revenue is Predictable -- If You Plan for It

HVAC is one of the most seasonal businesses that exists. The automation system accounts for this by building pre-season ramp-up campaigns that start generating demand 4-6 weeks before each peak. When other companies are scrambling to fill their schedule in October, yours is already booked because the furnace reminder campaign fired in September.

Emergency Calls Cannot Wait Until Morning

A furnace dying at 11 PM on a Thursday in January is not a lead that can sit in a queue until Monday. The system includes after-hours routing that captures the call, sends an instant confirmation to the customer, and alerts the on-call tech -- all within 60 seconds. The customer knows help is coming. The tech knows the details. Nobody has to check voicemail in the morning and hope the customer has not already called someone else.

Maintenance Plans Are the Real Money

Every HVAC owner knows that recurring maintenance agreements are the highest-margin, most predictable revenue in the business. The system automates the entire lifecycle: initial offer after a service call, renewal reminders, seasonal check-in scheduling, and upsell opportunities when equipment is aging. It turns one-time repair customers into multi-year maintenance clients without your office staff having to track a single spreadsheet.

Your Competitors Are Getting Found First

When someone searches "AC repair near me" on Google -- or asks ChatGPT "who is the best HVAC company in [your city]" -- your business needs to show up. The build includes full SEO optimization, Google Business Profile strategy, and AI-readable site files that position you where customers are actually looking. Not where they looked five years ago.

Common Questions

How does AI automation handle seasonal HVAC demand? +

The system tracks historical call volume and market signals to automatically ramp up lead generation and marketing before each season hits. In spring, it shifts messaging toward AC tune-ups and new installs. In fall, it pivots to furnace inspections and heating maintenance. During peak demand, automated follow-up sequences ensure no lead falls through the cracks while your techs are slammed with service calls. Off-season, the system runs maintenance reminder campaigns to fill your schedule when it would otherwise be empty.

Will this work with my existing CRM and scheduling tools? +

Yes. Every build is custom-integrated with whatever tools you already use. If you are on ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, or any other field service platform, I build the automation layer on top of your existing stack. If you do not have a CRM yet, I set one up as part of the build. The goal is zero disruption to your current operations -- the automation enhances what you already have rather than replacing it.

How quickly will I see results? +

Most HVAC companies see measurable improvement within the first 30 days. Automated follow-up sequences typically recover 15-25% of leads that were previously falling through the cracks. Review generation systems start building social proof immediately after the first completed job. The full compounding effect -- where SEO, reviews, follow-ups, and marketing all reinforce each other -- usually takes 60-90 days to fully ramp.
