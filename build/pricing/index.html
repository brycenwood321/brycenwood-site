Common Questions

Why start with a diagnosis instead of a build?+

Because most owners ask for the wrong first system. In 90 minutes I can see where your money actually leaks, and the plan tells you what to build first. If you then have me build it within 30 days, the diagnosis is free.

Do I own what you build?+

Yes. Everything runs in your own accounts: your CRM, your hosting, your Claude login. Nothing is locked to me.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. I don't. Everything I run, I built by talking to Claude Code in plain English, and that is how you will run it too.

What if I want changes after a build?+

You get 14 days of fixes after hand-off. After that you can run it yourself, hire me for a change, or take the retainer.
