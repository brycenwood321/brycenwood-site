## Everything that must match, address first

 Open your Google listing on one side and your website on the other. Tick a line only when it matches exactly. Your ticks stay on this device.

 
 
 
### Google listing vs your website

 **Address (do this one first)**Street number, street name, suite or unit, city, ZIP. A missing suite number or an old address after a move is a mismatch. &ldquo;Ste&rdquo; versus &ldquo;Suite&rdquo; is fine.
 **Business name**The same words on both. No extra keywords added to the Google name.
 **Phone number**The same number on both, and it is the one that actually rings.
 **Hours**Every day of the week, including the days you are closed. Then any holiday hours.
 **Categories**Your main Google category says the same thing your home page headline says you do.
 **Services**The services on your listing are on your website, with the same names.
 **Website link**The link on your listing opens a real page, and it loads on a phone.
 **Service area**The towns on your listing are towns your website says you serve.
 
 
 
### Then the other places people look

 **Apple Maps**Same address, phone and hours.
 **Facebook page**Same address, phone and hours.
 **Yelp and Bing Places**Same address, phone and hours, if you have them.

## Let AI find every difference

 Copy everything you can see on your Google listing, then your website's footer, contact page and home page headline. Paste both into the prompt in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. It lists every mismatch, worst first, and the one to fix first.

 
 
Compare my Google listing with my website, line by line, and tell me everything that does not match exactly.

Check these, in this order:
1. Address: street, suite or unit number, city, ZIP. Treat "Ste" vs "Suite" or "S" vs "South" as a style difference, but flag a different number, a missing suite, or an old address as a real mismatch.
2. Business name: exactly as written, with no extra words on either side. "&" vs "and" is a style difference.
3. Phone number.
4. Hours, day by day, including any holiday or special hours.
5. What we do: my Google primary category and other categories, next to how my website&#x27;s home page title and headline describe the business.
6. The website link on the listing: does it go to a real page that matches the listing?

For each line, give me: MATCH, STYLE DIFFERENCE, or MISMATCH, then what each side says. Put the mismatches at the top. End with the one fix to make first. Do not guess at anything I did not paste. If a piece is missing, say "not provided".
