Four steps, ten minutes

1

Copy your last ten reviews

From Google, Facebook, wherever you get them. Keep the star rating with each one. Take the names out if you like. The prompt does not need them.

2

Paste them into the prompt

Copy the prompt below into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and paste your reviews where it says.

3

Read the patterns, not the single reviews

The prompt only counts something as a pattern if it shows up in at least two reviews. One-offs go in their own short list at the end, so one angry customer does not set your to-do list.

4

Do one thing with it

Fix the complaint that keeps coming up. Then take the praise that keeps coming up and put it on your home page, in your customers' own words.

The prompt

The exact review prompt

Works in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. No setup.

Below are my last ten customer reviews. Read them together, not one at a time, and tell me:

1. WHAT PEOPLE KEEP PRAISING. Every theme that shows up in two or more reviews. For each one: how many reviews mention it, and one short quote.
2. WHAT KEEPS COMING UP AS A COMPLAINT. Anything negative, or a wish, even when it is said politely ("only thing I'd change...", "a little pricey but..."). Same format: how many, one short quote.
3. ONE THING TO FIX and ONE THING TO SAY LOUDER in my marketing, based only on these reviews, with the reason for each in one sentence.

Rules:
- Only call something a pattern if it shows up in at least two reviews. Things mentioned once go in a short "mentioned once" list at the end.
- Use only what is in the text. Do not invent, and do not soften a complaint.
- If there are no repeated complaints, say that plainly instead of stretching to find one.
- Leave reviewer names out of your answer.
