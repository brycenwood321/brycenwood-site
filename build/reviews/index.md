## The exact review prompt

 Works in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. No setup.

 
 
Below are my last ten customer reviews. Read them together, not one at a time, and tell me:

1. WHAT PEOPLE KEEP PRAISING. Every theme that shows up in two or more reviews. For each one: how many reviews mention it, and one short quote.
2. WHAT KEEPS COMING UP AS A COMPLAINT. Anything negative, or a wish, even when it is said politely ("only thing I&#x27;d change...", "a little pricey but..."). Same format: how many, one short quote.
3. ONE THING TO FIX and ONE THING TO SAY LOUDER in my marketing, based only on these reviews, with the reason for each in one sentence.

Rules:
- Only call something a pattern if it shows up in at least two reviews. Things mentioned once go in a short "mentioned once" list at the end.
- Use only what is in the text. Do not invent, and do not soften a complaint.
- If there are no repeated complaints, say that plainly instead of stretching to find one.
- Leave reviewer names out of your answer.
