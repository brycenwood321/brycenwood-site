# I Don't Write Code. I Build Businesses.

 80+ automations. $300K+ in revenue. Zero lines of code written. Here's how.

 
## The Short Version

 I'm Brycen Wood. I run a vehicle wrap company called Summit Wraps with my business partner Landon in Utah. I've never written a line of code in my life. But in the last year, I've built 80+ business automation systems using Claude Code -- and took our company from $52K to $300K in revenue without hiring a single new employee.

 Now I build these same systems for other businesses.

 
## How This Started

 Summit Wraps had the same problems every small business has. Leads came in randomly. Follow-ups were manual. There was no CRM, no outreach system, no content pipeline. We were two guys in a shop doing great work but drowning in everything around it.

 I didn't have $10K for an agency. I didn't have $8K/month for a developer. So I started talking to Claude Code -- describing what I needed in plain English -- and it built it.

 First it was a simple lead scoring script. Then an email drafter. Then a CRM integration. Then a content pipeline. Then an SEO system. Then a dashboard I could check from my phone. Then a DM engine. Then a voice AI receptionist. One system at a time, each one solving a real problem I was dealing with that week.

 Within a few months, our two-person shop was operating like a company with a marketing team, a sales team, and an IT department. Except it was still just me and Landon.

 
## What I Learned

 The thing nobody tells you about building with AI is that the hard part isn't the code. The code writes itself. The hard part is knowing what your business actually needs. That's the part I'm good at -- I've been running businesses, not writing software. I know what it feels like when leads slip through the cracks, when you're doing the same task for the 500th time, when you know there's a better way but you don't know how to build it.

 The gap in the market isn't "people who can code." It's people who understand business problems deeply enough to describe the right solution -- and then use AI to build it in hours instead of months.

 That's the entire game. Know the problem. Describe the fix. Let the AI build it. Ship it. Move on to the next one.
