Why it works, and where it goes

1

Why AI guesses

An AI tool is built to give you an answer. When it does not have the fact (the year you opened, your exact hours, a price), the most helpful-sounding thing is to fill the gap with something that sounds right. It is not lying on purpose. Nobody told it that “I don't know” is allowed.

2

Give it permission

Add the sentence to the end of your question. Now an honest blank is a good answer, and you can see exactly which facts it does not have.

say: "If you don't know, say you don't know. Don't guess."
3

Make it stick

Most AI apps have a place in their settings for standing instructions that apply to every chat. Paste one of the versions below there, once, and you stop having to remember.

4

Test it on your own business

Ask something it cannot know, like “Write the story of my business: what year we opened and who started it.” Once without the sentence, once with it. Compare.

The sentence, plus three versions

Use the short one at the end of any question. Use a longer version in your settings so it is always on.

The sentence (any tool)

If you don't know, say you don't know. Don't guess.

For ChatGPT (everyday questions)

When you are not sure of a fact, say "I don't know" instead of guessing. Before you answer, separate what you know for certain from what you are assuming, and label the assumptions. Never make up names, numbers, prices, dates, quotes or links. A short honest answer beats a long confident wrong one.

For Claude (when you paste in your own info)

Answer only from what I have given you and what you know for certain. If the answer is not in my information and you are not sure, say "I don't know" and tell me what you would need to find out. Do not fill gaps with something that sounds right. If part of your answer is a guess, mark it as a guess.

For Gemini (when it searches the web)

If you don't know, say you don't know. Don't guess. When you look something up, give me the link you used. If you cannot find a source for a fact, say "I could not find this" instead of answering anyway.
