# Run your business on Claude Code.

 Five weeks, one evening a week, 8 to 12 business owners. You leave with your business written into files your AI reads every day, and one system of your own running. First cohort starts in December or January. Dates go to the people on the list first.

 [Hold me a seat](#seat)

 
## The five weeks

 
 WEEK 1**Build the house.**Plus your Setup Day that week. Claude Code installed, your folders, your standing rules. You leave with it working on your own machine.

 WEEK 2**The interview and the files.**Your business written down in files Claude reads every session, so you stop explaining yourself every time. Memory working.

 WEEK 3**The plan.**A ranked list of what to automate. You pick one and write its scope. A 20-minute one-on-one with me this week.

 WEEK 4**Build your first system.**A daily brief, a follow-up drafter or a quote drafter, running on your real data.

 WEEK 5**Make it stick, and show it.**The habits for coming back, one scheduled job, and a 3-minute demo to the room.

 

 
## Who it is for

 
 
 - **For:** business owners who want to run their own system, not hand it off.

 - **The guarantee:** come to all five sessions and the Setup Day, and if you leave without a working system, you get your money back. Change your mind before week 1 ends and you get a full refund, no questions.
 - **Included:** a cohort group chat for the five weeks and a 20-minute one-on-one in week 3.
 - **After:** graduates keep coming to Claude Code Night, the free Monday room.
