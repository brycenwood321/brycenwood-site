That is normal. The install call runs an interview about your business, and your assistant proposes three builds from your answers. We pick together on the first call.

Does it work on Windows?+

Yes. Claude Code runs on Mac and Windows. I am on a Mac, so the install call is where we sort out anything Windows-specific.

Can I get a refund?+
