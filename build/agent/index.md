## Five steps, about 15 minutes

 
 
 1
 
 
### Claim your business first

 Go to chatgpt.com/business-hub and claim your listing. The agent lives on your claimed business. I walked through claiming it in [my last video](/found/).

 
 
 
 2
 
 
### Open Agent builder and let it read your site

 It drafts the whole agent from your website: what you sell, your hours, your areas, your FAQs. Mine took about a minute.

 
 
 
 3
 
 
### Read every line

 It wrote most of mine well, but it is writing about your business from the outside. Delete anything that is not true or not current. Check every price and every link.

 **Your words, not its guesses.** If a price or a rule is wrong here, your agent says it to customers.

 
 
 
 4
 
 
### Paste the rules and fill in the blanks

 Copy the fill-in rules below, paste them at the very end of the Instructions box, and replace every part in [BRACKETS] with your own details. My own rule is below too, so you can see a finished one.

 **Once your rules are in, leave them.** I did not press &ldquo;Draft with ChatGPT&rdquo; again after adding mine, so nothing could write over them.

 
 
 
 5
 
 
### Test it, then publish

 Click Test in ChatGPT and ask it the five questions below. If an answer guesses, promises a result, or says something is safe for someone, fix the rule and ask again. Then publish.

### The fill-in rules (paste at the end of Instructions)

**Help them get started**
When a customer is ready to buy or book, give them this link: [YOUR ORDER OR BOOKING LINK] and mention [YOUR DEADLINE OR NEXT STEP, for example "orders close Friday at midnight"]. Never promise [THE RESULT YOUR CUSTOMERS WANT, for example weight loss, a ranking, a cure], or any health, money or legal result.

**Only say what is published**
If the answer is not on [YOUR WEBSITE] or in your business info, say so and point them to [YOUR CONTACT PAGE OR PHONE]. Never guess a price, a date, or whether you serve their area.

**Safety questions go to a human**
Never tell a customer that something is safe for them, allergen free, or right for their situation. Point them to [YOUR ALLERGEN, SAFETY OR POLICY PAGE] and tell them to contact [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] before they buy.

**No accounts, no actions**
Do not claim to place orders, change accounts, issue refunds, or contact staff. For anything about their own account, point them to [YOUR CONTACT OR FAQ PAGE].
