Questions & Answers

Is it really free? +

The free tier is free forever. Premium content with full walkthroughs and live sessions will be $97/month once we have enough content to justify it -- but founding members get first access.

Do I need coding experience? +

No. Everything is designed for non-technical business owners. If you can describe what your business needs, you can follow the builds.

What's the difference between the community and the custom build service? +

The community teaches you to build yourself (DIY). The custom build service is me building it for you (done-for-you). Many people start in the community and hire me when they realize what's possible.

How is this different from YouTube tutorials? +

YouTube gives you concepts. The community gives you the exact prompts, the exact code, the exact templates -- plus a place to ask questions when you get stuck.
