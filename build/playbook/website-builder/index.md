 The short prompts from the videos
 The PDF has the long April prompt. With Claude&rsquo;s newer model the prompt got a lot shorter. These are the exact words I typed in the videos, nothing added.

 1. The 40-page website remake (56 words)

 Before you paste: put a copy of your current website in a folder called `my-current-website` and a screenshot of your home page next to it, named `my-current-website.png`. Open Claude Code in the folder that holds both. Mine built 43 pages in about 36 minutes. No site yet? Use the long prompt in the PDF instead.

 Rebuild my current website (the folder my-current-website, home page pictured in my-current-website.png) as a 40-page site that looks more premium than this. Every service gets its own page, plus blog posts and the files that help ChatGPT and Google recommend me. Plan it, split the work, and check every page before you hand it to me.Copy the website prompt
 2. A whole ordering business from one paragraph

 Before you paste: make a text file called `my-business.txt` with your menu, prices and pickup or delivery info, and open Claude Code in that folder. Swap &ldquo;meal prep company&rdquo; for what you sell. Mine took about 18 minutes. Treat what you get as a first draft: it does not take card payments yet, and the recipe amounts on the shopping list are the AI&rsquo;s guesses until you correct them.

 I run a meal prep company. Here's my menu, prices and pickup info: my-business.txt. Build the whole business: a website people can order from, and an owner side that turns every week's orders into a shopping list and a prep sheet. Make it look like a real brand.Copy the business prompt
