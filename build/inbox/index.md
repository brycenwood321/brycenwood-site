## Copy, paste, see what slipped

 Tested on a sample inbox before it went on this page: it found the deadline, the money and the waiting customer, skipped the newsletter, and sent nothing.

 
 
 1Connect your email, or paste it in.
 2Paste the prompt.
 3Answer the top three tonight.
 
 
Go through my email from the last 30 days and find everything that slipped. I run a business and I am busy, so the important stuff gets buried under the noise. You are looking for the boring things nobody followed up on.

Rules first:
- Nothing sends without me. Do not send, reply, archive, delete or label anything. Read only. You may write drafts for me to look at, nothing more.
- Never make up a fact. If you are not sure whether I already answered, say so.
- Skip newsletters, receipts, ads, and automatic notifications unless one of them needs me to do something.

Find every email where:
1. Someone asked me a question or for something, and I never replied
2. Someone offered me something with a date or a deadline (an appointment, an inspection, a meeting, a spot, a feature)
3. Money is involved: someone I owe, someone who owes me, an invoice, a payment that failed
4. A customer or a lead is waiting on me
5. A free opportunity I never answered (press, a partnership, an introduction, a referral)

For each one, give me:
- Who it is and what they want, in one line
- How many days it has been waiting
- Why it matters (money, a deadline, a customer, an opportunity)
- A short reply I could send, in a plain, friendly voice

Put the most urgent first: anything with a deadline that has not passed yet, then money, then customers, then everything else. Stop at the 10 most important. If you find nothing in a category, say "nothing found" instead of filling space.

At the end, ask me which ones I want you to draft as real email drafts. Do not send anything.
