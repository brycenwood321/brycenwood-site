## Five that matter, five you can ignore

 
 
 1
 
 
### Important: giving it your real business info

 Before you ask it for anything, tell it how your business actually works. Use the fill-in note below, then paste it into a ChatGPT Project or a Claude Project so it is there every time.

 **What I said:** your pricing, your hours, and how you do things. That is what makes it useful.

 Important
 
 
 
 2
 
 
### Important: paying the $20 a month

 Pay for one month of the paid plan of whichever one you picked and use it every day. If you have not opened it by the end of the month, cancel it.

 **What I said:** the free one is a completely different tool.

 Important
 
 
 
 3
 
 
### Important: talking to it instead of typing

 Tap the microphone in the app and talk the way you would to a new hire. Messy is fine. It sorts it out.

 **What I said:** I talk to it way more than I type, even in the truck or when I am cooking dinner.

 Important
 
 
 
 4
 
 
### Important: checking what it actually did

 When it says something is done, open it and look. Then paste the check prompt below and make it list what it did not finish.

 **What I said:** it will tell you it is done when it is not.

 Important
 
 
 
 5
 
 
### Important: letting it run things while you sleep

 Start with one job on a schedule, like a summary of yesterday waiting for you each morning. Get one working before you add a second.

 **What I said:** mine runs jobs every single night, and when I wake up there is a report waiting for me.

 Important
 
 
 
 6
 
 
### Not important: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

 Pick one and stay with it for three months. Learning one well beats switching every time a new one wins the week.

 **What I said:** they pass each other every single month. Pick one and learn it.

 Not important
 
 
 
 7
 
 
### Not important: knowing how to code

 Describe what you want in plain English, the way you would explain it to a person. Ask it to explain anything you do not follow.

 **What I said:** I literally have zero coding experience.

 Not important
 
 
 
 8
 
 
### Not important: paying for ten AI tools

 Look at what you paid for last month and cancel anything you did not open. Keep the one you actually use.

 **What I said:** one AI that you actually use beats ten that you don&rsquo;t.

 Not important
 
 
 
 9
 
 
### Not important: letting it write all your posts

 Talk your idea out first, then let it clean up your words. If you want it to write, give it ten of your own past posts to learn from.

 **What I said:** unless you have trained it very well, people can usually tell.

 Not important
 
 
 
 10
 
 
### Not important: AI replacing you

 Use the last prompt below to find the parts of your week you hate, and start with the first one.

 **What I said:** it is replacing the parts of your day that you hate.

 Not important

### Your business, in one note

Here is how my business works. Use this every time you help me, and ask me before you guess anything that is not on this list.

Business: [NAME], [WHAT YOU DO] in [TOWNS YOU SERVE]
Hours: [YOUR HOURS]
Prices: [YOUR MAIN PRICES OR PRICE RANGES]
How a job goes, start to finish: [3 TO 5 STEPS]
What we never do: [THINGS YOU SAY NO TO]
Who my best customers are: [ONE OR TWO LINES]
How I talk to customers: [FOR EXAMPLE: SHORT, FRIENDLY, NO PRESSURE]
 Copy the note
 
 
 
### Make it check its own work

Before I trust this, go back over what you just did and answer plainly:
1. What exactly did you change or make? List each one.
2. What did you NOT finish, skip, or guess at?
3. How can I see it myself? Tell me where to look.
If anything is unfinished, say so first. Do not tell me it is done unless I can open it and see it.
 Copy the check
 
 
 
### Find the task you hate most

Ask me about my work week, one question at a time, until you know the tasks I put off, dread, or do over and over. Then give me the three that would save me the most hours if you helped with them, and for the first one, tell me exactly what to paste into you tomorrow morning to start.
