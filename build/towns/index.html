Four rules before you start

1

One town, one page

Not a line on your homepage. A real page, with its own address on your site, and the town name in the title and the main headline.

2

Make every page real

A real job you did there, how long it takes you to get there, something you actually know about the area. Pages that are the same with only the town name swapped look like copies. Google's own spam rules call those doorway pages, and they can do more harm than good.

3

Keep your real address

Each page lists the address you actually work from, exactly as it is on your Google listing. Never claim an office in a town you only drive to.

4

Link to every town page

Add each one to your services or service area page, and to your sitemap, so Google can find it.

Copy and paste

The template, and the prompt that fills it

Fill the template in yourself, or paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini (or Claude Code inside your website's folder) and answer its questions.

The template

Page title (the tab name): [What we do] in [Town], [State] | [Business name]
Web address: /[what-we-do]-[town]/
Main headline: [What we do] in [Town]
First two sentences (answer first): [Business name] does [what we do] for [homes / businesses] in [Town]. We are based in [your real town], about [X minutes] away, and we [come to you / you come to us].
Section, What we do in [Town]: [2 to 4 services, one line each, said the way a customer would say it]
Section, A recent job in [Town]: [one real job: what it was, the street or area if the customer is OK with it, one photo]
Section, Where in [Town] we work: [areas or landmarks you really work near]
Section, Questions people in [Town] ask: [3 questions with short, true answers, e.g. "Do you charge extra to come to [Town]?"]
Section, Contact: [Business name], [address exactly as on your Google listing], [phone], [hours]

The fill-it-in prompt

Fill in this town page template for my business. Ask me one question at a time for anything you need. Do not invent jobs, reviews, distances, neighborhoods or facts about the town. If I do not give you something, leave the [brackets] in so I can see what is missing.

First ask me: my business name, what we do, my real address, my phone, the towns I serve, and for EACH town: a real job we did there (with permission to mention it), how long it takes us to get there, and any local detail I actually know (neighborhoods we work in, a common problem there).

Then write one page per town using the template below. Every page must be different in substance, not just the town name swapped. If I do not have anything real for a town yet, tell me, and do not write that page.
