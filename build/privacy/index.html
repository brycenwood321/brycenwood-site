Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This policy explains what information brycenwood.com collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. Written in plain English because nobody reads legalese.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Fill out a contact or intake form (name, email address, business name, and any details you share about your business)
  • Sign up for a free resource like the Claude Code Business Playbook
  • Book a discovery call
  • Join the community waitlist

We also collect basic analytics data automatically when you visit the site, including pages viewed, referral source, device type, and general location (country/region). This data is aggregated and not tied to your identity.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Deliver resources you requested (playbooks, guides, etc.)
  • Communicate about services you expressed interest in
  • Send community updates if you opted in
  • Improve the site based on aggregate analytics data

We do not sell your information. We do not rent your information. We do not share your email list with anyone.

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate this site:

  • GoHighLevel -- CRM and form handling. When you submit a form, your information is stored in GoHighLevel so we can follow up with you. Their privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) -- Anonymous site analytics to understand traffic patterns. No personally identifiable information is sent to Google. Their privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare -- Hosting, CDN, and security. Cloudflare processes requests to serve the site and protect against abuse. Their privacy policy.

Cookies

This site uses a minimal number of cookies:

  • Google Analytics cookies -- Used to distinguish unique visitors and sessions. These are first-party cookies set by the GA4 script.
  • Basic functionality cookies -- Standard cookies required for the site to function (e.g., Cloudflare security cookies).

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Unsubscribe from any emails at any time using the unsubscribe link included in every message
  • Request a copy of the personal data we have on file for you
  • Request deletion of your personal data from our systems
  • Ask questions about how your data is being used

To exercise any of these rights, email brycen@brycenwood.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as it is needed to provide the services you requested. If you ask us to delete your data, we will do so promptly and confirm when it is complete.

Changes to This Policy

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. For significant changes, we will notify you by email if we have your contact information.

Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy? Reach out directly:

brycen@brycenwood.com
