## How It Works

Fill out a short form about your business -- what you do, where you're located, how you get customers, what's holding you back, and where you want to go. AI analyzes your answers and builds a custom starter system tailored to your specific situation.

This is not a generic template. Every output is different because every business is different.

---

## The 12 Questions

1. **Business Name** -- What's your business called?
2. **What does your business do?** -- Describe your products/services and who you serve.
3. **Business Location** -- City and state.
4. **Website URL** -- Optional. If provided, we'll scan it and include specific fixes.
5. **How do you currently get customers?** -- Referrals, Google/SEO, social media, paid ads, cold outreach, word of mouth, or other.
6. **What's your biggest bottleneck right now?** -- Be specific. The more detail, the better your starter system will be.
7. **Monthly Revenue Range** -- Pre-revenue, under $5K, $5K-$15K, $15K-$30K, $30K-$50K, $50K-$100K, or $100K+.
8. **12-Month Revenue Goal** -- Where do you want to be in a year?
9. **What tools/software do you currently pay for?** -- Optional. Helps us recommend systems that integrate with what you already use.
10. **What's the ONE thing you'd automate first?** -- If you could wave a wand and automate one part of your business, what would it be?
11. **Do you use social media for business? Which platforms?** -- Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, or none.
12. **Email Address** -- Where we'll send your personalized starter system.
