1 Make your business readable to AI

An llms.txt file is a plain-text cheat sheet that tells AI exactly what you do, where, and who for. This is the single fastest win, ~20 minutes.
Create an llms.txt file at yourdomain.com/llms.txt
Include who you are, what you do, where you serve, key services, and contact
Add an llms-full.txt with deeper detail for engines that want more
Link the exact pages you want AI to prioritize when it quotes you
Keep it factual and plain. No marketing fluff, AI ignores hype

2 Write content that answers what people ask AI

AI quotes the source that answers the question most directly. Keywords are out. Real questions are in.
List the 20 real questions customers ask before buying (questions, not keywords)
Write one clear answer page per question: "Best [service] in [city]," "How much does X cost," "X vs Y"
Put the answer in the first two sentences of the page. AI lifts the direct answer
Write like a human talking, not keyword-stuffed SEO copy
Add FAQ schema so engines can parse the Q&A cleanly

3 Get cited where AI already trusts

AI recommends businesses it sees mentioned across sources it already trusts. Citations are the proof.
Claim and fully complete your Google Business Profile
Get listed in your industry's authority directories
Earn reviews with volume, recency, and your business name in the text
Get mentioned on local and industry sites AI scrapes (press, partners, guest posts)
Keep your name, address, phone identical everywhere online

4 Measure it (and keep measuring)

You can't improve what you don't check. This compounds month over month.
Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini for the best [your service] in [your city]
Note the result: are you named, is a competitor named, or no one? All three tell you something
Re-check monthly. Watch your name start showing up as the work compounds
