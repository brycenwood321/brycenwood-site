## The exact churn prompt

 Works in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. No setup.

 
 
I want to find out whether something I changed made customers leave. I will give you two lists.

LIST 1, CHANGES: every change I made to the business, with the date. Prices, hours, menu or services, staff, policies, delivery or pickup, the website, how we bill. Small ones count.
LIST 2, WHO LEFT: every customer who cancelled, paused or stopped coming, with the date they left and anything I know about them (how they bought, what they bought, how long they had been a customer). First names or initials are fine.

Then:
1. Put everything on one timeline, oldest first, changes and departures together.
2. For each change, count how many people left in the 30 days before it and the 30 days after it.
3. Look at who left after each change. Do they have something in common (same way of buying, same product, same length as a customer)?
4. Tell me the one change most worth a closer look, and how sure you are: strong, weak, or too few people to tell.
5. Give me one question I could ask a customer who left, to check whether you are right.

Rules:
- Say "after", never "because". A timeline can show a pattern. It cannot prove a cause.
- If the numbers are too small to mean anything, say so plainly.
- Do not invent dates, customers or reasons. If something is missing from my lists, ask me for it before you start.
